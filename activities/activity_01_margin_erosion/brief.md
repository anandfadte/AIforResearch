# The Brief

> Align on the CFO's decision and decompose it into researchable questions. (~3 minutes)

## research_mode
Research Framing | Decomposing a vague executive symptom into testable analytical questions and hidden assumptions

## scenario

Your client is a global consumer goods company. Over the last two quarters (Q2 to Q4), consolidated gross margin dropped by **2.5 percentage points** — from **43.0% down to 40.5%**. Overall revenue held roughly steady, yet profit shrank.

The CFO presents to the board in 48 hours and needs a clear answer: **Where exactly did the 2.5 percentage points go?** Was it rising input costs, pricing pressure, product mix shifts, currency headwinds, or something else — and how much of it can actually be fixed?

Her mandate to your pod: *"Don't bring me theories or a wall of numbers. Show me where the margin went, what we control, and what we should do first."*

## context

You will receive a 4-file data pack in Phase 2. Before touching any files, align as a group on the exact decision the CFO needs to make and design the research questions that will drive your investigation.

## margin_bridge

Think of a margin bridge as a step-by-step waterfall explaining how profit margin moved from 43.0% to 40.5%. Every driver adds or subtracts percentage points (pp) until the math reconciles to −2.5pp, followed by what leadership can fix vs. what is structural.

## do_this

1. **Read the brief aloud in your pod.** Agree in one sentence: what decision does the CFO actually need to make? Write it down before opening any AI tool.
2. **Use your AI tool (Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT, etc.) to break the brief into 4 to 6 researchable analytical questions.** Push back on its first list — generic business questions won't help you interrogate real P&L data.
3. **Rank and select your top 2 questions** — the two that, if answered with hard numbers, would carry the board recommendation.

## prompting_guideline

Assign the AI a **critical research role** (for example, a skeptical finance research director rather than a helpful assistant). Paste the **full scenario brief verbatim** instead of summarizing it, and ask it to surface **testable research questions and hidden assumptions** — not premature answers. When the first list feels textbook or generic, push back directly and ask for sharper, data-testable questions.

## tip

You do not need a finance background for this sprint. Focus on the analytical logic: total sales stayed roughly flat while profit fell by 2.5 percentage points. Every analytical question you choose should help separate **what changed** (mix, input costs, pricing, currency) from **what is fixable**.

## human_decision

Your AI tool can brainstorm research angles, but **only your pod** can decide which question the CFO is truly paying you to answer. Pick your top two questions and record them before moving on.

## checkpoint

Can every member of your pod state the CFO's core decision and your two priority research questions in one clear sentence without reading notes? If not, align now before unlocking Phase 2.
