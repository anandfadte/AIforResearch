# The Deliverable

> Create something the CFO could take straight into a board meeting. (~7 minutes)

## research_mode
Executive Communication | Packaging evidence-backed analysis into a board-ready one-page margin bridge

## context

**Your group's output: a one-page margin bridge.** This is a simple visual that breaks down the 2.5 percentage point margin drop into its components (e.g., pricing, volume, product mix, costs, currency). For each component, show whether it is fixable or structural (something the company has to live with). End with your top recommendation and its expected impact.

## do_this

1. **Brief your AI tool clearly.** Tell it exactly what you need: "Create a one-page margin bridge analysis for a CFO board presentation. The audience is the board of directors. Break down the 2.5pp margin drop into components. For each component, state the margin impact in percentage points and whether it is fixable or structural. Include our top recommendation with expected margin recovery. Use our three insights: [paste your insights here]."
2. **Review and iterate.** The first draft will not be perfect. Ask your AI tool to revise specific parts — tighten the language, fix any numbers that do not match your analysis, remove any claims you cannot support with evidence.
3. **Fact-check line by line.** Go through the final output together. Every number must trace back to your data. Any claim you cannot verify — remove it.
4. **Choose a presenter.** One person will share the group's output and one thing the AI tool got wrong during the process.

## prompting_guideline

Brief your AI tool the way you would brief a junior analyst: tell it the audience, the decision the output supports, your three insights with evidence, your recommendation, and the exact format. Say: "Do not add any claims or numbers beyond what I have given you." Then iterate — ask for specific changes rather than starting over.

## tip

Keep it to one page. Board members and senior leaders do not read long documents. If you cannot explain your finding in two or three sentences, it is not clear enough yet. Ask your AI tool: "Simplify this to something a non-financial board member would understand in 30 seconds."

## human_decision

Your name goes on this deliverable, not the AI's. Before you present, every group member should read it one final time — silently and critically.

## checkpoint

Could the CFO take this into a board meeting and present it without asking you a single clarifying question? That is the standard.
