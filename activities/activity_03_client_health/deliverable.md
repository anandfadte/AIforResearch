# The Deliverable

> Create an assessment the account leader could take straight into a client review meeting. (~7 minutes)

## research_mode
Executive Communication | Packaging client health analysis into a stakeholder-ready assessment with prioritised actions

## context

**Your pod's output: Client health assessment.**
A one-page assessment for the account leader: the real state of the account (by service line, not blended), what's driving the score drop, whether this is a service problem or a perception/stakeholder problem, and your recommended action plan — specific, prioritized, and distinguishing between urgent fixes and structural changes.

## do_this

1. **Brief your AI tool clearly.** Tell it exactly what you need: "Create a one-page client health assessment for an account leader. Show satisfaction scores by service line (not blended). Identify what is driving the CSAT drop from 8.2 to 6.4. Distinguish between service problems and perception problems. Include a prioritised action plan. Use our three insights: [paste your insights here]."
2. **Review and iterate.** The first draft will not be perfect. Ask your AI tool to revise specific parts — tighten the language, fix any numbers that do not match your analysis, remove any claims you cannot support.
3. **Fact-check line by line.** Go through the final output together. Every score and claim must trace back to your data. Anything you cannot verify — remove it.
4. **Choose a presenter.** One person will share the group's output and one thing the AI got wrong during the process.

## prompting_guideline

Brief the AI the way you would brief a junior team: the audience, the decision the output must support, your three insights with their evidence, your recommendation, and the exact format. Ban it from adding any claim you have not supplied, and iterate; the first draft is never the last.

## human_decision

Your name goes on this, not the AI's. The last read before you present is human, silent, and skeptical.

## tip

Keep it to one page. Account leaders have five other accounts to worry about. If you cannot explain each finding and its recommended action in one sentence, it is not clear enough yet. Ask your AI tool: "Simplify this so an account leader would understand it in 30 seconds."

## checkpoint

Could the account leader take this into a client review meeting and present it without asking a single clarifying question? That is the standard.
