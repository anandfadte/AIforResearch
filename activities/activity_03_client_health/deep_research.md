# Deep Research

> Interrogate the data and triangulate against the outside world. (~8 minutes)

## research_mode
Analytical Deep-Dive | Cross-referencing satisfaction scores against support tickets, survey response rates, and stakeholder changes to separate signal from noise

## context

Your pod now has its triaged sources and two priority research questions. In this phase, you will interrogate the Excel data systematically — profiling it first, then testing your hypotheses against hard numbers.

## do_this

1. Give the Excel to your AI tool. Ask for a profile first — row counts, blanks, duplicates, inconsistent labels — before any analysis. Clean what needs cleaning and note what you changed.
2. Ask the AI to test your pod's two key questions against the data. Demand the workings, not just the answer.
3. Use AI web research plus the documents in your pack to bring in outside evidence. Where do the data and the published sources agree? Where do they collide?

## prompting_guideline

Make the AI profile before it analyzes: row counts, blanks, duplicates, inconsistent labels, implausible values. Have it list every issue and wait for your instructions; never let it draw conclusions from data neither of you has inspected.

## prompting_guideline_2

When a finding starts to feel true, prompt for the opposite: ask the AI to find credible, recent evidence that would contradict it, and to say plainly if it finds none. And for any number it produces, demand the workings, not just the answer.

## human_decision

Every claim your pod carries forward needs two independent supports, or an explicit label: hypothesis. AI will happily give you one confident source. You decide what counts as enough.

## checkpoint

Did the data profile change anything about your analysis? If the answer is "we didn't profile it", go back — that step exists for a reason.
