# The Brief

> Align on the account leader's decision and decompose it into researchable questions. (~3 minutes)

## research_mode
Research Framing | Decomposing an emotional escalation into testable client health questions and hidden assumptions

## scenario

A services client's satisfaction scores have dropped from 8.2 to 6.4 over the past six months. The relationship manager is in panic mode — he's heard the client mention "market testing" and wants an emergency response. The account leader is calmer but concerned: "Before we throw service credits and executive visits at this, I need to understand what's actually happening. Is the whole account deteriorating, or is something specific going on? And is this a real service problem or a perception problem?"

Your pod needs to diagnose the situation using the satisfaction data, support ticket records, and two additional sources. The account leader wants a clear-eyed assessment, not a panic response.

## context

You will receive a 4-file data pack in Phase 2. Before touching any files, align as a group on the exact decision the account leader needs to make and design the research questions that will drive your investigation.

## do_this

1. **Read the brief aloud in your pod.** Agree in one sentence: what decision does the account leader actually need to make? Write it down before opening any AI tool.
2. **Use your AI tool (Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT, etc.) to break the brief into 4 to 6 researchable analytical questions.** Push back on its first list — generic client-health questions won't help you interrogate real satisfaction data.
3. **Rank and select your top 2 questions** — the two that, if answered with hard numbers, would carry the account recommendation.

## prompting_guideline

Strong prompts in this phase assign the AI a critical role (a skeptical research director, not a helpful assistant), give it the full brief verbatim rather than your summary of it, and ask for questions and hidden assumptions instead of answers. If its first list feels generic, say so and push for sharper.

## tip

You do not need a client management background for this sprint. Focus on the analytical logic: satisfaction dropped from 8.2 to 6.4, but is that blended score hiding very different stories across service lines? Every question you choose should help separate **real service failure** from **measurement noise** and **stakeholder-driven perception shifts**.

## human_decision

Your AI tool can brainstorm research angles, but **only your pod** can decide which question the account leader is truly paying you to answer. Pick your top two questions and record them before moving on.

## checkpoint

Can every member of your pod state the stakeholder's decision in one sentence, without looking at notes? If not, you are not aligned yet.
