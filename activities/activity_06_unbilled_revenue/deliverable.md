# The Deliverable

> Create a revenue leakage report the CFO could take straight into the earnings prep. (~7 minutes)

## research_mode
Executive Communication | Packaging reconciliation findings into a board-ready leakage report with recovery priorities

## context

**Your group's output: a one-page revenue leakage report.** This shows the total gap ($4.2M), broken down by root cause, with each category labelled as recoverable, partially recoverable, or structural. End with your top recommendation and the expected revenue recovery.

## do_this

1. **Brief your AI tool clearly.** Tell it exactly what you need: "Create a one-page revenue leakage report for a CFO earnings prep. Show the $4.2M gap broken down by root cause category. For each category, state the dollar amount, whether it is recoverable, and the recommended action. Include our top recommendation with expected recovery amount and timeline. Use our three insights: [paste your insights here]."
2. **Review and iterate.** The first draft will not be perfect. Ask your AI tool to revise specific parts — tighten the language, fix any numbers that do not match your reconciliation, remove any claims you cannot support.
3. **Fact-check line by line.** Go through the final output together. Every dollar amount must trace back to your data. Any claim you cannot verify — remove it.
4. **Choose a presenter.** One person will share the group's output and one thing the AI tool got wrong during the process.

## prompting_guideline

Brief your AI tool the way you would brief a junior analyst: tell it the audience, the decision the output supports, your three insights with evidence, your recommendation, and the exact format. Say: "Do not add any claims or numbers beyond what I have given you."

## tip

Keep it to one page. The CFO is preparing for an earnings call — she needs a narrative, not a spreadsheet. If you cannot explain each leakage category in one sentence, it is not clear enough yet.

## human_decision

Your name goes on this deliverable, not the AI's. Before you present, every group member should read it one final time — silently and critically.

## checkpoint

Could the CFO use this report to explain the revenue gap to analysts on the earnings call without asking you a single clarifying question? That is the standard.
