## tip

The reflection matters as much as the deliverable. Revenue leakage is a recurring problem — the process you build to detect it matters more than catching this quarter's gap.

## reveal_distractors

**Finance VP Email:** The email blamed account managers for "not submitting hours on time." This is true for about $380K of the gap (9%). The email takes a real but small problem and presents it as the explanation. If your group accepted the timesheet narrative, you missed the larger systemic causes.

## reveal_quirks

**47 delivery records with project codes that don't exist in the billing system** — these are projects from an acquisition completed 8 months ago. The acquired company's project codes were never migrated into the billing system. They represent $1.1M of the gap and are the single largest category — but only if you noticed the code mismatch.

**Three "premium support" service lines are billed quarterly in arrears, not monthly** — if you flagged these as unbilled, they are not actually leakage. They will appear on next quarter's invoices. This is roughly $420K that looks like leakage but is not.

## reveal_insight_path

The leakage breaks into three categories: (1) **Unmigrated acquisition projects** ($1.1M) — delivery records exist but project codes don't map to the billing system. This is fully recoverable once codes are mapped. (2) **Scope creep on fixed-fee contracts** ($1.6M) — additional work delivered beyond contract scope that was never change-ordered or invoiced. Partially recoverable, requires client conversations. (3) **Time-and-materials underreporting** ($1.1M) — billable hours recorded in the delivery system but not submitted to billing, primarily in two service lines with manual timesheet processes. Recoverable with process fix. The remaining $400K is timing (quarterly billing) and genuine write-offs.

## reveal_takeaway

Within the next 7 days, run one reconciliation from your day job — any pair of systems that should agree but might not — through the same profiling and matching process with your AI tool. The technique of systematic record matching will surface gaps you didn't know existed.
