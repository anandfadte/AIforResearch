# The Brief

> Align on the CFO's question and decompose it into researchable billing hypotheses. (~3 minutes)

## research_mode
Research Framing | Decomposing a revenue leakage symptom into testable hypotheses about billing process failures

## scenario

Your client is a professional services firm with 340 active client accounts. An internal audit flagged that **$4.2 million in delivered services went unbilled last quarter** — roughly **6.8% of total quarterly revenue**. The gap has been growing: it was 3.1% two quarters ago and 4.9% last quarter.

The CFO needs a clear answer before the earnings call: **Where is the leakage, and is it getting worse?** Is it specific clients, service lines, or contract types — and how much can be recovered?

Her mandate to your pod: *"The auditors found the gap. I need you to tell me why it exists, where it's concentrated, and what we bill tomorrow morning."*

## context

You will receive a 4-file data pack in Phase 2. Before touching any files, align as a group on the exact question the CFO needs answered and design the analytical approach that will drive your reconciliation.

## do_this

1. **Read the brief aloud in your pod.** Agree in one sentence: what decision does the CFO actually need to make? Write it down before opening any AI tool.
2. **Use your AI tool (Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT, etc.) to identify 4 to 6 possible causes of billing leakage in a professional services firm.** Push back on the first list — generic billing issues won't help you reconcile real delivery-vs-invoice data.
3. **Rank and select your top 2 hypotheses** — the two that, if confirmed with data, would explain the largest share of the $4.2M gap.

## prompting_guideline

Assign the AI a **revenue assurance role** (for example, a forensic accountant rather than a helpful assistant). Paste the **full scenario brief verbatim** and ask it to surface **testable hypotheses about where billing breaks down** — not generic best practices. When the first list feels like a textbook, push back and ask for hypotheses that can be tested by reconciling delivery logs against invoices.

## tip

Revenue leakage often hides in the handoffs between teams. Delivery teams mark work as complete; billing teams generate invoices. If the two systems don't talk to each other perfectly, work falls through the cracks. Your job is to find those cracks.

## human_decision

Your AI tool can brainstorm leakage hypotheses, but **only your pod** can decide which hypothesis the CFO needs answered for the earnings call. Pick your top two and record them before moving on.

## checkpoint

Can your pod explain in one sentence where you think the leakage is concentrated and why? If not, refine your hypotheses.
