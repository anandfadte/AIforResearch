# Source Triage

> Read every file once before trusting any of it. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Source Auditing | Evaluating what each document actually is, who made it, and whether it has an agenda before it enters the analysis

## context

Download all four files and read through each one before you start analysing anything. The most credible-looking document in a people analytics pack is often the most misleading.

## files

- Workforce_Snapshot.xlsx
- Exit_Survey_Data.xlsx
- Compensation_Analysis.xlsx
- CHRO_Email_Thread.docx

## do_this

1. Open each file and write one line about it: what it is, who made it, and what it is trying to show.
2. For each file, ask three things: Is it recent? Does it cover the right people and time period? Does the author have a stake in a particular interpretation?
3. As a group, decide which files you will rely on, which you will treat with caution, and which you are setting aside.

## prompting_guideline

For each file, ask your AI: "Is this current, does it cover the right population, and is there any reason the author might have a stake in a particular conclusion? What is the strongest reason to doubt it?"

## checkpoint

Has your group flagged at least one document as potentially biased or limited in scope? At least one of these files was written to support a point of view rather than present balanced evidence. If everything looks equally credible, look again.
