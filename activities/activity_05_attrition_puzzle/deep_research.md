# Deep Research

> Profile the data, test your hypotheses, and find the signal the exit surveys are hiding. (~10 minutes)

## research_mode
Analytical Deep-Dive | Joining workforce and compensation data to find where attrition is actually concentrated, and testing whether the narrative in the qualitative sources holds up

## do_this

1. Profile the workforce data before drawing conclusions. Know what you have before you interpret it.
2. Test your two research questions against the data. Where is attrition actually concentrated?
3. Bring in the compensation data. Where does the salary gap appear, and for which employees?
4. Cross-reference with the exit survey. Where do the numbers and the stated reasons align, and where do they diverge?

## prompting_guideline

Profile before you analyse. Ask your AI to list every data quality issue it finds (blank fields, inconsistent labels, anything that looks off) and wait for your review before drawing conclusions. When a pattern starts to emerge, test it from the other direction: ask what the data looks like if you are wrong. If the exit survey and the workforce data point in different directions, your group decides which one to trust more and why. An explanation that holds up under challenge is the one worth taking to a board.

## checkpoint

Do you have at least two independent pieces of evidence for your main finding? If only one source is pointing in a direction, treat it as a hypothesis until you can verify it.
