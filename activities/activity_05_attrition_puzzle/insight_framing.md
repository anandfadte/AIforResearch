# Insight Framing

> Turn your findings into something the CHRO can actually act on. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Evidence Synthesis | Compressing quantitative findings into decision-ready insight statements that name the problem, size it, and point toward action

## do_this

1. Turn each finding into one sentence: what you found, why it matters, and what should happen next. Aim for three to five.
2. Take them to your AI tool and say: "Act as a skeptical board member. For each of these insights, tell me: is this a genuine insight or a restated fact? Where is the evidence thin? What question would you ask that we cannot currently answer?"
3. Keep your three strongest insights. Cut anything that would be equally true for any company in any industry.

## prompting_guideline

Give the AI a specific edge when stress-testing. Say: "You are a CFO who has seen three bad people analytics reports this year. For each insight, tell me what is missing, where the evidence is weakest, and what you would challenge if this came to a board meeting." Three sharp insights are worth more than eight generic ones.

## checkpoint

Read your three insights aloud. If any of them could apply to a company in a completely different industry without changing a word, sharpen it or cut it.
