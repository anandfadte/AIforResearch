# The Brief

> Align on the COO's question and decompose it into researchable process hypotheses. (~3 minutes)

## research_mode
Research Framing | Decomposing an operational symptom into measurable process stages and testable delay hypotheses

## scenario

Your client is a mid-market e-commerce company that ships 12,000 orders per week from two warehouses. Over the last quarter, average order-to-delivery time has gone from 4 days to 11 days. Customer complaints are up 340%, and the NPS score dropped from +38 to +9.

The COO needs answers before the board meeting on Friday: where exactly is the time going? Is it warehouse picking, packing, carrier handoff, last-mile delivery, or something else entirely, and what can be fixed within 30 days?

Her mandate to your pod: "Show me the data. Which stage is broken, how much time it adds, and what we do first."

## context

You will receive a 4-file data pack in Phase 2. Before touching any files, align as a group on the exact question the COO needs answered.

## do_this

1. Read the scenario out loud. As a group, write one sentence: what decision does the COO actually need to make? No AI tools until everyone agrees.
2. Map out the fulfilment process and generate 4 to 6 testable delay hypotheses. Push back if the first list feels like a textbook.
3. Pick your top 2 hypotheses. These are the delay points that, if confirmed with data, would explain the majority of the 7-day blowout.

## prompting_guideline

Give your AI a process analyst role rather than a helpful assistant role. Paste the full scenario and ask it to surface testable hypotheses about where delay accumulates in the fulfilment chain. When the first list comes back, push on it: what would you need to find in the timestamp data to confirm or rule out each one?

## checkpoint

If someone outside your group asked "where do you think the 7 days went?" could you answer in one sentence right now? "Somewhere in the warehouse" is not specific enough. A named stage and a reason is.
