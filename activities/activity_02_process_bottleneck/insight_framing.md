# Insight Framing

> Turn your findings into a clear operational story. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Evidence Synthesis | Converting stage-level delay data into an actionable narrative with quantified fix priorities

## context

You now have data showing where the delay lives. Your job is to frame this as a clear story the COO can act on: not a data dump, but a prioritised list of what to fix and in what order.

## do_this

1. Turn each finding into one sentence: which stage, how many days, what root cause, and whether it is fixable within 30 days. Aim for three to five.
2. Take them to your AI tool and ask it to rank by impact and ease of fix. Push back if the ranking puts only the easiest fixes at the top.
3. Keep your three strongest insights. The COO cares about days recovered, speed of fix, and customer impact.

## prompting_guideline

Frame each insight as the bottleneck, the root cause, and the recommended action. Ask your AI to draft a one-sentence statement for each that a COO could read and immediately know what to do, including the quantified impact in days. When stress-testing the ranking, ask whether the highest-impact fix might be worth extra effort even if it takes longer.

## checkpoint

Read your three insights aloud. Would a COO hearing them for the first time know exactly what is broken and what to do? If not, sharpen them.
