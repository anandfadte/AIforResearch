# The Deliverable

> Create a savings plan the CTO could take straight into the board meeting. (~7 minutes)

## research_mode
Executive Communication | Packaging cost analysis into a board-ready waste map and 90-day savings plan

## context

**Your group's output: a one-page cloud savings plan.** This shows the total waste identified, broken down by category, with each category assigned to a 90-day implementation wave. End with your top recommendation and the expected monthly savings.

## do_this

1. **Brief your AI tool clearly.** Tell it exactly what you need: "Create a one-page cloud savings plan for a CTO board presentation. Show the cost-usage gap (42% cost growth vs. 18% usage growth). Break down the waste into categories with monthly dollar amounts. Organise into three implementation waves (Week 1, Month 1, Month 3). Include our top recommendation with expected monthly savings. Use our three insights: [paste your insights here]."
2. **Review and iterate.** The first draft will not be perfect. Ask your AI tool to revise specific parts — tighten the language, fix any numbers that do not match your analysis, remove any claims you cannot support.
3. **Fact-check line by line.** Go through the final output together. Every dollar amount must trace back to your data. Any claim you cannot verify — remove it.
4. **Choose a presenter.** One person will share the group's output and one thing the AI tool got wrong during the process.

## prompting_guideline

Brief your AI tool the way you would brief a junior analyst: tell it the audience, the decision the output supports, your three insights with evidence, your recommendation, and the exact format. Say: "Do not add any claims or numbers beyond what I have given you."

## tip

Keep it to one page. Board members do not read FinOps reports. If you cannot explain each waste category and its fix in one sentence, it is not clear enough yet. Ask your AI tool: "Simplify this so a non-technical board member would understand it in 30 seconds."

## human_decision

Your name goes on this deliverable, not the AI's. Before you present, every group member should read it one final time — silently and critically.

## checkpoint

Could the CTO take this into a board meeting and present it without asking you a single clarifying question? That is the standard.
