# Insight Framing

> Turn your findings into a clear revenue recovery story. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Evidence Synthesis | Converting reconciliation findings into an actionable leakage report with recovery priorities

## context

You now have data showing where the $4.2M leakage lives. Your job is to frame this as a clear story the CFO can act on: distinguishing recoverable revenue from structural leakage and prioritising what to bill first.

## do_this

1. Turn each finding into one sentence: how much leaked, why, and whether it is recoverable. Aim for three to five.
2. Take them to your AI tool and ask it to rank by dollar amount and speed of recovery. Push back if everything lands in the "recover immediately" bucket.
3. Keep your three strongest insights. Distinguish what can be billed this week from what requires a client conversation.

## prompting_guideline

Frame each insight as the amount, the root cause, and the recommended action. Ask your AI to draft a one-sentence statement for each that a CFO could read and immediately know what to do, including the dollar amount and whether it is recoverable. When stress-testing the recovery timeline, ask about client relationship risk: some of this revenue may be months old and clients may dispute late invoices.

## checkpoint

Read your three insights aloud. Would a CFO hearing them for the first time know exactly how much money is at stake and what to do about it? If not, sharpen them.
