# The Brief

> Align on the CFO's question and decompose it into researchable billing hypotheses. (~3 minutes)

## research_mode
Research Framing | Decomposing a revenue leakage symptom into testable hypotheses about billing process failures

## scenario

Your client is a professional services firm with 340 active client accounts. An internal audit flagged that about $4.2 million in delivered services went unbilled last quarter, roughly 6.8% of total quarterly revenue. The gap has been growing: it was 3.1% two quarters ago and 4.9% last quarter.

The CFO needs a clear answer before the earnings call: where is the leakage, and is it getting worse? Is it specific clients, service lines, or contract types, and how much can be recovered?

Her mandate to your pod: "The auditors found the gap. I need you to tell me why it exists, where it's concentrated, and what we bill tomorrow morning."

## context

You will receive a 4-file data pack in Phase 2. Before touching any files, align as a group on the exact question the CFO needs answered.

## do_this

1. Read the scenario out loud. As a group, write one sentence: what decision does the CFO actually need to make? No AI tools until everyone agrees on the sentence.
2. Generate 4 to 6 possible causes of billing leakage in a professional services firm. Push back if the first list feels like generic best practices.
3. Pick your top 2 hypotheses. These are the causes that, if confirmed with data, would explain the largest share of the $4.2M gap.

## prompting_guideline

Give your AI a revenue assurance role rather than a helpful assistant role. Paste the full scenario and ask it to surface testable hypotheses about where billing breaks down. When the first list comes back, push on it: what would you need to find by reconciling delivery logs against invoices to confirm or rule out each one?

## checkpoint

Can your pod explain in one sentence where you think the leakage is concentrated and why? If not, refine your hypotheses.
