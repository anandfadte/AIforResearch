# Insight Framing

> Turn your findings into a clear revenue recovery story. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Evidence Synthesis | Converting reconciliation findings into an actionable leakage report with recovery priorities

## context

You now have data showing where the $4.2M leakage lives. Your job is to frame this as a clear story the CFO can act on — distinguishing recoverable revenue from structural leakage and prioritising what to bill immediately.

## do_this

1. **Distil your findings into 3 insights.** Each insight should follow this pattern: "[$X.XM] leaked because [root cause]. This is [recoverable / structural / needs contract renegotiation]."
2. **Ask your AI tool to help you build a recovery timeline.** Upload your findings and ask: "Rank these leakage categories by: (1) dollar amount recoverable, (2) speed of recovery (can we invoice this week?), (3) likelihood of client pushback. Which should the CFO action first?"
3. **Challenge the recovery plan.** If the AI suggests invoicing everything immediately, ask about client relationship risk. Some of this revenue may be months old — clients may dispute late invoices.

## prompting_guideline

Frame your insights as **"$X leaked because Y, therefore Z"** — the amount, the root cause, the recommended action. Ask your AI tool: "For each leakage category, draft a one-sentence insight statement that a CFO could read and immediately know what to do. Include the dollar amount and whether it is recoverable."

## tip

The CFO cares about three numbers: how much leaked, how much can be recovered, and how fast. Frame everything in those terms. A $4.2M problem where $2.8M is recoverable this quarter is a very different conversation from one where only $600K can be recovered.

## human_decision

Your group must decide what to recommend billing immediately vs. what requires a client conversation first. The AI can suggest, but your pod owns the call.

## checkpoint

Read your three insights aloud. Would a CFO hearing them for the first time know exactly how much money is at stake and what to do about it? If not, sharpen them.
