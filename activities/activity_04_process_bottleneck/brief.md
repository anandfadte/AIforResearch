# The Brief

> Align on the COO's question and decompose it into researchable process hypotheses. (~3 minutes)

## research_mode
Research Framing | Decomposing an operational symptom into measurable process stages and testable delay hypotheses

## scenario

Your client is a mid-market e-commerce company that ships 12,000 orders per week from two warehouses. Over the last quarter, average order-to-delivery time has **blown out from 4 days to 11 days**. Customer complaints are up 340%, and the NPS score dropped from +38 to +9.

The COO needs answers before the board meeting on Friday: **Where exactly is the time going?** Is it warehouse picking, packing, carrier handoff, last-mile delivery, or something else entirely — and what can be fixed within 30 days?

Her mandate to your pod: *"I don't want opinions or anecdotes. Show me the data — which stage is broken, how much time it adds, and what we do first."*

## context

You will receive a 4-file data pack in Phase 2. Before touching any files, align as a group on the exact question the COO needs answered and design the analytical approach that will drive your investigation.

## do_this

1. **Read the brief aloud in your pod.** Agree in one sentence: what decision does the COO actually need to make? Write it down before opening any AI tool.
2. **Use your AI tool (Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT, etc.) to decompose the order fulfilment process into 5 to 7 measurable stages.** Push back on its first list — generic process maps won't help you interrogate real timestamp data.
3. **Identify your top 2 hypotheses** — the two delay points that, if confirmed with data, would explain the majority of the 7-day blowout.

## prompting_guideline

Assign the AI a **process analysis role** (for example, a Six Sigma black belt rather than a helpful assistant). Paste the **full scenario brief verbatim** and ask it to surface **testable hypotheses about where delay accumulates** — not generic process improvement advice. When the first list feels like a textbook, push back and ask for hypotheses that can be tested with timestamp data.

## tip

You do not need an operations background for this. Focus on the logic: if 12,000 orders per week used to take 4 days and now take 11 days, 7 days of delay appeared somewhere in the chain. Your job is to find where those 7 days live and whether each cause is fixable.

## human_decision

Your AI tool can generate process hypotheses, but **only your pod** can decide which hypothesis matters most for the COO's Friday board meeting. Pick your top two and record them before moving on.

## checkpoint

Can your pod explain in one sentence where you think the delay is hiding and why? If not, refine your hypotheses.
