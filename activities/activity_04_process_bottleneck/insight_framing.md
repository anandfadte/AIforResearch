# Insight Framing

> Turn your findings into a clear operational story. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Evidence Synthesis | Converting stage-level delay data into an actionable narrative with quantified fix priorities

## context

You now have data showing where the delay lives. Your job is to frame this as a clear story the COO can act on — not a data dump, but a prioritised list of what to fix and in what order.

## do_this

1. **Distil your findings into 3 insights.** Each insight should follow this pattern: "[Stage X] adds [N days] of delay because [root cause]. This is [fixable within 30 days / structural / needs further investigation]."
2. **Ask your AI tool to help you prioritise.** Upload your findings and ask: "Rank these bottlenecks by: (1) days of delay removed if fixed, (2) ease of fix within 30 days, (3) customer impact. Which should the COO tackle first?"
3. **Challenge the ranking.** If the AI ranks the easiest fix first, ask whether the highest-impact fix might be worth the extra effort. The COO wants results, not easy wins.

## prompting_guideline

Frame your insights as **"X because Y, therefore Z"** — the bottleneck, the root cause, the recommended action. Ask your AI tool: "For each bottleneck, draft a one-sentence insight statement that a COO could read and immediately know what to do. Include the quantified impact."

## tip

The COO does not care about process maps or swim lanes. She cares about: how many days can we recover, how fast, and at what cost. Frame everything in those terms.

## human_decision

Your group must decide the recommended sequence of fixes. The AI can suggest, but your pod owns the recommendation.

## checkpoint

Read your three insights aloud. Would a COO hearing them for the first time know exactly what is broken and what to do? If not, sharpen them.
