# The Deliverable

> Create a bottleneck map the COO could take straight into the board meeting. (~7 minutes)

## research_mode
Executive Communication | Packaging process analysis into a board-ready bottleneck map with quantified fix priorities

## context

**Your group's output: a one-page bottleneck map.** This is a visual that shows each process stage, the delay it contributes (in days), the root cause, and whether it is fixable within 30 days. End with your top recommendation and its expected impact on delivery time.

## do_this

1. **Brief your AI tool clearly.** Tell it exactly what you need: "Create a one-page bottleneck analysis for a COO board presentation. Show each process stage from order to delivery. For each stage, show: baseline cycle time, current cycle time, delay added, root cause, and whether it is fixable within 30 days. Include our top recommendation with expected recovery in days. Use our three insights: [paste your insights here]."
2. **Review and iterate.** The first draft will not be perfect. Ask your AI tool to revise specific parts — tighten the language, fix any numbers that do not match your analysis, remove any claims you cannot support with evidence.
3. **Fact-check line by line.** Go through the final output together. Every number must trace back to your data. Any claim you cannot verify — remove it.
4. **Choose a presenter.** One person will share the group's output and one thing the AI tool got wrong during the process.

## prompting_guideline

Brief your AI tool the way you would brief a junior analyst: tell it the audience, the decision the output supports, your three insights with evidence, your recommendation, and the exact format. Say: "Do not add any claims or numbers beyond what I have given you." Then iterate — ask for specific changes rather than starting over.

## tip

Keep it to one page. Board members do not read long documents. If you cannot explain the bottleneck and the fix in three sentences, it is not clear enough yet. Ask your AI tool: "Simplify this so a non-operations board member would understand it in 30 seconds."

## human_decision

Your name goes on this deliverable, not the AI's. Before you present, every group member should read it one final time — silently and critically.

## checkpoint

Could the COO take this into a board meeting and present it without asking you a single clarifying question? That is the standard.
