# The Brief

> Align on what the CHRO actually needs before anyone opens a spreadsheet. (~3 minutes)

## research_mode
Research Framing | Breaking a people-problem symptom into testable hypotheses about where attrition is concentrated and what is driving it

## scenario

You are working with the CHRO of a 650-person technology services company. Over the past 12 months, voluntary attrition has gone from 8 percent to 18 percent — more than doubling. The board meets in 48 hours.

Everyone in the leadership team has a theory. The CHRO thinks it is the return-to-office policy that came into effect in January. Sales leaders think it is compensation. Several managers have flagged career development. Nobody has looked at the data properly.

Your task is to find out where people are leaving from, why they are leaving, and what the company can realistically fix in the next 90 days. The CHRO does not want theories — she needs a retention brief she can take to the board.

## context

You will receive a data pack in Phase 2. Before opening any files, your group needs to agree on the question you are actually trying to answer.

## do_this

1. Read the scenario aloud in your group. Agree in one sentence on what the CHRO needs to decide in the next 48 hours. Write it down before opening any AI tool.
2. Use your AI tool to generate 4 to 6 specific, data-testable research questions. Push back on the first list if it feels generic — "why do people leave companies" is not useful here.
3. Pick your top 2 questions. These should be the ones that, if answered with hard numbers from the data, would directly inform the retention brief.

## prompting_guideline

Give your AI tool a research director role, not a helpful assistant role. Paste the full scenario and ask it to identify testable hypotheses about where attrition is likely concentrated and what factors are most plausible. When the first list comes back, challenge it: what would you need to find in the data to confirm or rule out each one?

## human_decision

Your group decides which two questions you are actually trying to answer. The AI can generate options, but the selection is yours — and it determines everything that follows.

## checkpoint

Can every person in the group state the CHRO's core question and your two priority research questions in a single sentence without reading notes? If not, align now before moving to Phase 2.
