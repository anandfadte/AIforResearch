# Source Triage

> Read every file once before trusting any of it. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Source Auditing | Evaluating what each document actually is, who made it, and whether it has an agenda before it enters the analysis

## context

Download all four files and read through each one before you start analysing anything. In people analytics, the most credible-looking documents are often the most misleading. Someone's sincere belief about why attrition is happening is not the same as evidence.

## files

- Workforce_Snapshot.xlsx
- Exit_Survey_Data.xlsx
- Compensation_Analysis.xlsx
- CHRO_Email_Thread.docx

## source_lens
1. Current?|Was this created recently, and does it reflect the situation as it is today?
2. Scope?|Does it cover the right people, the right time period, and the right question?
3. Agenda?|Does the author have a reason to interpret the data in a particular direction?

## do_this

1. Open each file and write one line about it: what it is, who created it, and what it is trying to show.
2. Run each file through the three-step verification lens. Use your AI tool to flag recency issues, scope limitations, and any reason the author might have a stake in a particular interpretation.
3. As a group, decide which files you will rely on, which you will treat with caution, and which you are going to set aside.

## prompting_guideline

For each document, tell your AI tool: "I am evaluating this source for a people analytics brief. Tell me: is this current, does it cover the right population, and is there any reason the author might have a stake in a particular conclusion? What is the strongest reason to doubt it?"

## human_decision

The decision to trust or discard a source is yours. At least one of these four files was written to support a particular point of view rather than present balanced evidence. It is worth identifying it before it shapes your analysis.

## checkpoint

Has your group flagged at least one document as potentially biased or limited in scope? If everything looks equally credible, look again.
