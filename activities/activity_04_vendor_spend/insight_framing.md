# Insight Framing

> Turn your findings into a clear savings story. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Evidence Synthesis | Converting cost analysis into an actionable savings plan with quantified 90-day targets

## context

You now have data showing where the waste lives. Your job is to frame this as a clear story the CTO can act on: not generic FinOps advice, but a specific savings plan with dollar amounts and timelines.

## do_this

1. Turn each finding into one sentence: monthly waste amount, root cause, and whether it is a quick win or requires engineering work. Aim for three to five.
2. Take them to your AI tool and ask it to organise into three waves: turn off now, right-size next month, renegotiate in 90 days. Push back if everything lands in wave one.
3. Keep your three strongest insights. CTOs think in monthly burn rate, not annual totals.

## prompting_guideline

Frame each insight as the monthly cost, the root cause, and the recommended action. Ask your AI to draft a one-sentence statement for each that a CTO could read and immediately know what to turn off or change. When stress-testing the plan, ask about implementation risk: turning off resources without understanding dependencies can cause outages.

## checkpoint

Read your three insights aloud. Would a CTO hearing them for the first time know exactly what to turn off and when? If not, sharpen them.
