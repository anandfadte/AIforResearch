# Insight Framing

> A finding is what the data says. An insight is why it changes the stakeholder's decision. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Synthesis & Challenge | Converting raw findings about satisfaction scores into decision-ready insights for the account leader

## context

Your pod has profiled the data, tested hypotheses, and triangulated across sources. Now distil the noise into three sharp insights the account leader can act on.

## do_this

1. Turn each finding into one sentence: what you found, why it matters, and what should happen next. Aim for three to five.
2. Take them to your AI tool and ask it to push back: is each one a genuine insight or a restated fact, and where is the evidence thin?
3. Keep your three strongest. Cut anything that would make equal sense for any client in any industry.

## prompting_guideline

Cast the AI as the toughest executive in the room and give it your drafts. Ask it to identify the weakest link in the evidence for each insight and what question it would ask that you cannot currently answer. The strongest client health insights distinguish between things that need immediate action and things that need investigation.

## checkpoint

Read your three insights aloud. If any of them would make sense for any client in any industry, it is too generic. Sharpen it or drop it.
