# Source Triage

> Audit every file in your pack for recency, scope, and motive before trusting any of it. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Source Auditing | Stress-testing provenance, recency, scope coverage, and author bias before letting data into the analysis

## context

Download each file and open everything before trusting anything.

## files

- Client_Satisfaction_Tracker.xlsx
- Support_Ticket_Summary.docx
- Relationship_Manager_Email.docx
- Industry_Benchmark_NPS.docx

## do_this

1. Open each file and write one line about it: what it is, who made it, and what it is trying to show.
2. For each file, ask three things: Is it recent? Does it cover the right service lines and population? Does the author have a stake in a particular interpretation?
3. As a group, decide which files you will rely on, which you will treat with caution, and which you are setting aside.

## prompting_guideline

For each file, ask your AI whether it is current, whether it covers the right service lines and population, and whether the author might have a stake in a particular conclusion. The strongest reason to doubt a source is as important as the reasons to trust it.

## checkpoint

Has your pod discarded or heavily discounted at least one source, in writing, with a reason you would say to its author's face?
