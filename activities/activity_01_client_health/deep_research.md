# Deep Research

> Interrogate the data and triangulate against the outside world. (~8 minutes)

## research_mode
Analytical Deep-Dive | Cross-referencing satisfaction scores against support tickets, survey response rates, and stakeholder changes to separate signal from noise

## context

Your pod now has its triaged sources and two priority research questions. Interrogate the data systematically: profile it first, then test your hypotheses against hard numbers.

## do_this

1. Profile the data before analysing it. Know what you have, including response rates and population coverage, before you interpret any scores.
2. Test your two research questions against the data. Where do the numbers actually point, and where is the signal unreliable?
3. Bring in the outside sources. Where do the data and the documents agree, and where do they conflict?

## prompting_guideline

Profile before you analyse. Ask your AI to list every data quality issue it finds (blank fields, low response rates, inconsistent labels) and wait for your review before drawing conclusions. When a finding starts to feel true, test it from the other direction: ask what the data looks like if you are wrong. For any claim it makes, ask to see the working, not just the answer.

## checkpoint

Did the data profile change anything about your analysis? If the answer is "we didn't profile it", go back.
