# The Brief

> Align on the account leader's decision and decompose it into researchable questions. (~3 minutes)

## research_mode
Research Framing | Decomposing an emotional escalation into testable client health questions and hidden assumptions

## scenario

A services client's satisfaction scores have dropped from 8.2 to 6.4 over the past six months. The relationship manager is in panic mode. He has heard the client mention "market testing" and wants an emergency response. The account leader is calmer but concerned: "Before we throw service credits and executive visits at this, I need to understand what is actually happening. Is the whole account deteriorating, or is something specific going on? And is this a real service problem or a perception problem?"

Your pod needs to diagnose the situation using the satisfaction data, support ticket records, and two additional sources. The account leader wants a clear-eyed assessment, not a panic response.

## context

You will receive a 4-file data pack in Phase 2. Before touching any files, align as a group on the exact decision the account leader needs to make.

## do_this

1. Read the scenario out loud. As a group, write one sentence: what decision does the account leader actually need to make? No AI tools until everyone agrees on the sentence.
2. Generate 4 to 6 specific, testable research questions about what might be driving the satisfaction drop. Push back if the first list feels generic.
3. Pick your top 2. These are the questions that, if answered with hard evidence, would directly shape the account assessment.

## prompting_guideline

Give your AI a research director role rather than a helpful assistant role. Paste the full scenario and ask it to generate testable hypotheses about what might be driving the satisfaction drop. When the first list comes back, push on it: what would you need to find in the data to confirm or rule out each one?

## checkpoint

Can every member of your pod state the account leader's decision in one sentence without looking at notes? If not, you are not aligned yet.
